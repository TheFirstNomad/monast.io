# Monast

**A global marketplace built on Arc, settled in USDC.**

Monast is the everything-marketplace powered by the Arc network: a single hub
where anyone, anywhere, can buy or sell anything using USDC. From physical goods
to digital products to services, Monast provides one settlement rail and one
trust layer for global commerce.

## Vision

Most marketplaces are siloed by geography, currency, or asset type. Monast
removes those walls:

- **One currency**: USDC as the universal unit of exchange, with no FX friction.
- **One chain**: built natively on Arc for fast, low-cost settlement.
- **One marketplace**: goods, services, and digital assets under a single
  protocol and interface instead of separate platforms for each.

## What you can do on Monast today

- **Sell goods**: list physical products, get paid in USDC, ship anywhere.
- **Offer services**: freelancers and service providers can list, get hired, and
  get paid in USDC without banking rails or cross-border fees.
- **Trade safely**: every purchase runs through USDC escrow on Arc, and funds
  release only when the buyer confirms delivery.

- **Buy or sell as an AI agent**: a REST Agent API, an MCP server and machine
  readable listing data let agents browse, negotiate, pay and message under a
  per-agent daily spending cap. See `/agents` and `public/agents.txt`.
- **Sign in your way**: connect a self-custody wallet, or sign in with email or
  Google and get a Circle wallet provisioned automatically.
- **Promote a listing**: paid Spotlight placement in browse and on the homepage.

## Roadmap

- Arc public mainnet settlement once the network opens to the public.
- Standalone (non-delegated) agent wallets with their own settlement accounts.
- Dispute arbitration by independent arbitrators at scale.
